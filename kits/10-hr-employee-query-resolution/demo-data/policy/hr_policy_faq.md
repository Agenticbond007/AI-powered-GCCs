# HR policy extract (SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA)

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA — fictional. Not a real company, person, invoice, bank account, tax identifier, employee, or payment. For playbook testing only.

- Payslips: published on the HR self-service portal on the pay date. HR does not email payslips to personal addresses.
- Leave beyond balance is treated as loss of pay (LOP) in the month it is taken.
- Reimbursements approved by the 20th are paid with that month's payroll.
- Investment proofs for the financial year are submitted on the HR portal in the window HR announces; the people desk shares the window from the HR calendar only.
- Salary bank account changes are made by the employee in HR self-service with OTP verification. The people desk cannot change bank details from email.
- Full and final settlement: processed after all exit clearances, within the timeline in the policy.
