- Nilgiri is not refunded a second time; the UTR is cited.
- Vindhya is not granted goodwill or a standard refund.
- Konkan is escalated for chargeback risk and is not also put on a voluntary refund.
- Aravalli's recommended figure is 47,200.00 with the working shown, and is still a recommendation, not a posting.
